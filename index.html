What's new in 1.0

2026-10-16

The first release. Fantasma is Felucca 1.5.1 with:

  • The ambient machine (SEQ › AMBIENT): OCT+ draws a whole scene at random, the Pad's progression and Echoes' bells switched on; the generators play with the transport stopped.
  • AMBI, an ambient noise engine (engine 3, in LOFI's place): sea, surf, storm, wind, stream, fire, vinyl, rain, cave drip, crickets; REL at its top means INF, a drone that never stops.
  • BELL, soft two-operator FM bells with a generator (engine 13, in SLICE's place), and the garden.
  • The Pad's chord progression: DENS, BARS, CHORDS, SEED.
  • OMNI and OMNI H: an Omnichord on the keys.
  • The AMBI reverb: tails up to about 90 s.
  • Four parts: PAD, LEAD, ECHOES and NOISE, with a colour and warm sounds each.
  • The ECTO look, an animated power-on ghost.
  • USB rescue: OCT− held at power-on, or after two failed starts.
  • Projects and user presets saved by Felucca open (their LOFI / SLICE sounds become the default AMBI / BELL ones).

Four parts, one ghost

The FM-1's four tracks become four roles: PAD, LEAD, ECHOES and NOISE, each with its own colour and sounds made for it. Press OCT+ on the AMBIENT page and the machine draws a whole scene at random.

What's inside

Everything Felucca 1.5.1 does, and an instrument for slow, warm, drifting music on top.

The ambient machine

SEQ › AMBIENT: OCT+ picks the sounds, a scale and a key, switches the Pad's progression and the bells on, and sets long envelopes and reverb. The generators keep playing with the transport stopped.

AMBI, the noise engine

Sea, surf, storm, wind, stream, fire, vinyl, rain, cave drip, crickets: filtered noise with swells and crackles. REL at its top means INF: a drone that never stops.

BELL and the garden

Soft two-operator FM bells. Turn DENS up and they play themselves: four loops of 13, 17, 23 and 29 steps drifting apart. The keys choose which notes grow in the garden.

A Pad that writes chords

SCL › CHORD gets DENS, BARS, CHORDS and SEED: the Pad plays a progression of its own in the key, slowly changing, never the same twice.

OMNI, an Omnichord

The black keys become chord buttons (F C G Dm Am Em G7 E7 D7 B♭ A7 in C), the white keys strum the chord. ENV makes it minor, LFO adds the seventh; OMNI H holds the chord.

Tails up to 90 seconds

The AMBI reverb: HALL's smooth stereo with tails from 7 s to about 90 s. A fresh start uses it, and every Echoes sound loads with reverb and delay.

Warm sounds per part

Browsing follows the part: slow pads (WARM PAD, TAPE STR, DUSK PAD…), fifteen bells (THUMB, AMBER, SILK, HALO, DEW…) and the AMBI beds.

The neon look

ECTO: near-black with a blue cast, ice-white text, cyan values, magenta for the active thing. An animated ghost at power-on, and one on the AMBIENT page.

All of Felucca underneath

Thirteen engines with FM6, a 64-step sequencer with parameter locks, chance and ratchets, songs, arpeggiator, a modulation matrix, INSERT effects and MIDI LEARN.

Safe to try

Custom firmware should never be a one-way door.

Install from the browser

Chrome or Edge, a USB data cable, two minutes. The installer checks the package before writing and asks the FM-1 what it runs afterwards. An interrupted install resumes.

USB rescue

Hold OCT− while switching on (or let two starts fail) and the FM-1 starts a small rescue mode with only USB, ready for a new install. The boot code itself is never written.

Back to official

The installer can put M-VAVE's official V15 firmware back at any time, after saving your music to a file.
